Key Market Trends



• Shift to On-Ground Properties: Regulatory crackdowns and market maturity have reduced unconstructed land files, with authorities and buyers favoring possession-ready plots and built houses.

• Lower Interest Rates: Following drops in benchmark interest rates down to around 11%–12%, borrowing conditions and investor confidence have improved compared to the peak rates of 2023.

• The Vertical Revolution: Due to land scarcity in central areas like Gulberg and DHA, high-rise luxury apartments and mixed-use commercial towers are rising, offering rental yields around 5% to 7%.

• Infrastructure Growth: Connectivity projects—such as the Lahore Ring Road corridors and developments like DHA Phase X (Phase 10) and Lahore Smart City—continue to anchor high-demand investment zones.

• Legal Scrutiny: The Lahore Development Authority (LDA) and proposed master plans are clamping down on unauthorized or green-zone housing schemes, making project verification critical before investing.
